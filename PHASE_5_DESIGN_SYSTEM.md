## 2. Typography & "Ink Pen Feel" Stacks
- **Header & Title:** `'Playfair Display', 'Cinzel', serif` (Editorial, archival).
- **Body / Spoken Transcript (The Ink Pen Feel):**
  - **Malayalam:** `'Chilanka', cursive` (Authentic, natural ballpoint script).
  - **Hindi:** `'Kalam', cursive` (Organic hand-lettered Devanagari script).
  - **English:** `'Caveat', 'Patrick Hand', cursive` (Natural student fountain pen cursive).
- **Polaroid Chin Captions:** Authentic handwritten script matching language.

---

## 3. Scrapbook & Picture Layout Engine
- **Mode 1 (Inline with Text):** Photos placed inside the flowing text with auto-wrapping.
- **Mode 2 (Collage & Ephemera Canvas):** Free placement of Polaroids, tickets, cafe receipts, and washi-tape stamps.
- **Mode 3 (Dedicated Image Page):** In the 1-to-5 page pagination sequence, a whole page can be dedicated purely to a 2x3 or 2x2 Polaroid grid.
- **Mode 4 ("Something Directly from the Page"):** Pinned photograph of a real handwritten paper scrap.

---

## 4. The "Torn Letter" Keepsake & Privacy Quarantine
- Perforated top-edge with simulated spiral-tear fringe.
- Cherry-picked memory snippet + birthday note.
- Direct export to WhatsApp / Instagram with 0% data leakage.

## 5. Microcopy & Zomato-Style Whispers
- **Playful Reality Check:** *"Quick question before you sleep... did today look like the life you've been manifesting, or are we hitting reset tomorrow morning?"*
- **The Reassurance:** *"Just checking in on you. That goal you wrote down a few weeks ago? It hasn't forgotten you either. One small step at a time."*
- **Late-Night Quiet Moment:** *"The world is quiet right now. Close your eyes for 5 seconds and picture that version of you who already achieved what you wrote. She's waiting for you 🌙"*
- **Fading Voice Nudge:** *"Some whispers from last month are fading into the quiet tonight... Want to listen one last time, or pin the ones you love? 🎧"*
